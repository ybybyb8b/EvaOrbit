SVG marks are vendored from [Simple Icons 13.21.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/13.21.0/icons), distributed under [CC0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/13.21.0/LICENSE.md). Brand names and marks remain the property of their respective owners. Used only to identify a user's subscription, without implying affiliation.

Accent colors follow the [published icon metadata](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/13.21.0/_data/simple-icons.json). OpenAI uses a monochrome treatment following its [current brand guidance](https://openai.com/brand/). Color is mixed into the theme surface; the logo itself retains its brand treatment. Unknown subscriptions use EvaOrbit theme tones and an initial.
